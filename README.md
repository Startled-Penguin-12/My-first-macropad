# My-first-macropad
A macropad. That I made. its versatile, open source (obviously!), and REALLY COOL. (Plz say its cool plz)

The PCB was designed in KiCAD. It includes 11 keys and an encoder switch wired in a row2col matrix (arbitrarily) and a 0.91in I2C OLED display.

The Case was made in tinkercad because I suck at 3d modeling and so does my computer. It's a really open case because
1. I like to look at the PCB spaghetti
2. I wanted most of the parts out in the open anyways (microcontroller buttons, diodes, OLED) that I figured if was easier to leave the case open
3. I will probably take the pcb out and fidget with it/take it outside
4. I MADE A PCB OFC I WANT TO SHOW IT OFF!!

The firmware is KMK becuase I heard it was easier to code...
